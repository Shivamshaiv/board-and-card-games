# Original artwork

The live courtyard combines original Three.js models, generated surface textures, and a baked panoramic horizon. Production images live in `public/art/`. The source panoramas and early illustrated concepts are kept in `art-sources/`. Regenerate the environment and reflection textures with `scripts/bake-panorama.py` and Pillow.

## Oasis materials

These are the generation prompts for the physical materials used by the 3D environment. The limestone is mapped in world units. The two transparent foliage images are bent over modeled branches and palm fronds. The aerial material follows the terrain of the far bank.

### limestone.webp

One perfectly seamless tileable physically based base-color material texture for a high fidelity real-time Egyptian Nile courtyard game: a perfectly orthographic top down macro photo scan of one continuous naturally weathered fine-grained pale golden cream Egyptian limestone surface. Soft mineral inclusions, rare tiny shell fossils and soft faint veins, granular calcite pores, worn stippled patina, small occasional slender hairline fissures; warm bone ivory and sand tan with slight mottled beige. Even flat overcast cross-polarized photographic lighting everywhere, absolutely no directional shadow, no perspective, no objects, no border, no labels, no mortar, no paving slab grid, no big high contrast patches, no graphical or illustrated style. The entire square image edge to edge represents a single 65 cm by 65 cm material scan that will repeat on carved architecture and cut pavers. Crisp realistic high quality albedo texture.

### palm-frond.webp

A single photoreal isolated feather-pinnate date palm FROND foliage texture, transparent background, all surrounding pixels transparent. Long ultrawide approximately 3:1 horizontal landscape composition: the thin woody cut base is exactly on left end, long tapered pointed TIP exactly on the right end. One elegantly slightly drooping central rachis, nearly horizontal precisely along vertical centerline of the image, gently tapering to a fine point. Leaf fan widest in left-middle, slowly tapers to end; delicate realistic narrow sword-shaped leaflets in approximately 38 pairs splaying upward/downward in a full symmetrical feather pattern, organic little bends, gaps with transparent alpha between strands. Dark botanical date-palm green at bases, olive and sage variation with warm golden-green sunlit leaf edges. Natural slightly waxy matte leaf surface with visible rib. Even soft daylight, no surroundings, no shadow, no writing, no borders, no cluster of stems, no tree trunk. For a 3D physically based cutout maple-style foliage card asset where the tip is on the right; all of the leaf included with tiny transparent margin. Natural, not cartoon.

### floodplain.webp

A realistic orthographic top-down aerial albedo TEXTURE for a detailed ancient Egyptian Nile floodplain game environment. Landscape 2:1 panorama, continuous seamless field material to wrap horizontally. Sun-warmed verdant cultivated Nile valley fields, small irregular cultivated rectangles of wheat, deep dusty sage orchards, rich olive date groves, ochre earth and green crop rows, intersecting narrow blue-green irrigation ditches, sandy earthen paths, patches of scrub. The frame depicts approximately 90 metres across, varied field sizes, fine lifelike atmospheric colors with amber edges and cool shadowed green; dry arid soil around the lush irregular farm patches. Directly perpendicular satellite view with no horizon, no sky, absolutely no big water body, no sea or large river, no modern roads or machinery or power poles, no writing, no decorative borders. The main Nile and nearby buildings will be modeled separately. Uniform even illumination for a photographic material texture, fine detail, archaeological Egyptian countryside.

### orchard-leaves.webp

Create one photographic tree foliage-cluster alpha texture for 3D modelling along the Nile, square transparent background. A dense spreading, rounded but ragged bough of a real African sycamore fig / olive tree, seen slightly from above in daylight. Many tiny individual dusty olive and sage leaves overlap, dark forest green inner shadows, soft silver-gold sunlit outer leaf edges. Approximately round irregular lush clump, little organic transparency gaps, subtly visible fine branching within, no visible trunk or ground or scene. Leaves should maintain a believable tree silhouette and look like a dense leafy cluster with hundreds of leaves when tiny on screen. Tight composition fills almost whole square leaving thin alpha margin, no border, no shadows outside the object. Photorealistic high-resolution game foliage cutout, naturally uneven asymmetrical silhouette, absolutely no background or text.

### nile-water.webp

A clean patch of water was cropped from the original generated `terrace-panorama.webp`. The water surface combines this material with a procedural ripple map and reflections from the surrounding sky.

### oasis-environment.webp and oasis-reflection.webp

`scripts/bake-panorama.py` projects the original cylindrical terrace artwork into a 129-degree sector of the surrounding sky and aligns its horizon with the modelled riverbank. It writes a 4096 × 2048 background and a 512 × 256 reflection map. Both assets ship with the game.
